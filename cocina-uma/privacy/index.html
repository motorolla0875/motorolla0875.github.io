Privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains what data Chef's Kitchen (also called Cocina Uma and Cozinha do Chef), a game by dmith.builds, uses.

The game does not collect, store on servers or share any personal data. It does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your email or name. It does not use your contacts, camera, microphone or location. It has no ads and no purchases, and it works offline.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, shop upgrades and settings such as music and language) is stored only on your device. If you delete the app, it is erased.

Children

We do not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game ever adds ads or another feature that uses data, we will update this policy and the date above before doing so.
